# Niranjan's Photo Stickers 📸

Drop Niranjan's real photos here to replace the default placeholder stickers!

## How to replace photos:
Simply replace the following files with Niranjan's photos:

1. `niranjan-1.png` — Left Top Sticker (The Birthday Boy 🎂)
2. `niranjan-2.png` — Left Middle Sticker (Main Character Energy ✨)
3. `niranjan-3.png` — Left Bottom Sticker (Bro Level: 100 👑)
4. `niranjan-4.png` — Right Top Sticker (Certified Legend ⭐)
5. `niranjan-5.png` — Right Middle Sticker (Happy Birthday! 🎈)
6. `niranjan-6.png` — Right Bottom Sticker (VIP Birthday Aura ⚡)

### Tips for best look:
- Transparent cutout PNGs work best!
- If the photos have normal backgrounds, the stickers automatically display them within stylish rounded scrapbook frames with white borders, washi tape, and drop shadows!
- You can also add or modify sticker labels and initial positions in `src/data/stickers.js`.
